BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things to know

About reading, listening, and your local library.

Which systems and file formats are supported?

QYReader runs on Windows 10 / 11. Import TXT, Markdown, Word (DOC / DOCX), EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF, HTML, and ZIP files. You can also paste text or extract a public web article into your local library.

Can I listen without an internet connection?

Yes. Download or import a Kokoro offline Chinese voice pack to generate and play speech locally, or use Windows system voices. Edge online voices need an internet connection and send the text being read to the speech service. Kokoro models are downloaded separately; synthesis speed depends on your computer.

Does the floating reader share my progress?

Yes. The floating reader and main window share the same reading position and playback state. Keep using it when the main window is minimized, or return to the main window. In text-only mode, the title bar and playback controls hide when the pointer moves away.

Where are my books, notes, and reading progress stored?

On your computer by default, with no account required. Your library, progress, and notes are not uploaded. Save a full snapshot in Settings → Backup & Restore. When using Edge online voices, the text being read is sent to that service.

Is the desktop interface available in English?

The current desktop interface is in Simplified Chinese, and the bundled Kokoro voice packs provide Chinese voices. This website and the English README introduce the app in English. The images on this page are UI design concepts in Chinese; the actual release may differ.
